Privacy Policy — Anvil Bastion

1. Developer and contact

Anvil Bastion is developed and published by BurningArktis.

Privacy and support contact: SUPPORT_EMAIL_PLACEHOLDER

2. Local game data

Anvil Bastion is designed as an offline-first game. Gameplay progress and settings are stored locally on the user's device. Local data may include currencies, equipment, research levels, stage progress, optional advertising reward counters, settings, and timestamps used for offline or time-based gameplay systems.

The game does not require users to create an account for core gameplay.

3. Optional rewarded advertising

Builds of Anvil Bastion that enable advertising may use Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads to provide optional rewarded advertisements. A rewarded advertisement is initiated by the user. The game grants the associated gameplay reward only when the advertising SDK reports that the reward was earned. Closing or skipping an advertisement, a load failure, or a show failure does not by itself grant the gameplay reward.

4. Information processed by Google Mobile Ads

When advertising is enabled, Google Mobile Ads may automatically collect and share information for advertising, analytics, and fraud-prevention purposes. According to Google's Mobile Ads documentation, this may include IP address, product interactions such as app launches or ad views, diagnostic/performance information, and device/account identifiers where applicable.

5. Consent and advertising privacy choices

Where required, Anvil Bastion uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to obtain or refresh consent information before requesting network advertisements. When UMP indicates that privacy options must be available, the app provides an in-app entry point for applicable advertising privacy choices.

6. Third-party processing

Advertising-related information may be processed by Google and advertising partners configured through Google AdMob. Google's privacy policy is available at policies.google.com/privacy.

The initial release does not require a separate user-account service, contact-list access, photo-library access, or precise-location feature for core gameplay.

7. Data retention and deletion

Local game data remains on the device until it is deleted by the user, for example by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app. Because the initial release does not require a BurningArktis account for core gameplay, BurningArktis does not maintain a separate server-side gameplay account database to delete.

8. Security

Local gameplay data is stored using the platform and application storage mechanisms available on the device. Advertising data handled by Google Mobile Ads is documented by Google as encrypted in transit. BurningArktis limits production advertising requests until the applicable privacy/consent state permits them.

9. Children and target audience

BurningArktis maintains the app's target-audience declaration and advertising configuration through Google Play and Google AdMob. If a released version includes children in its declared target audience, the app and advertising configuration will be reviewed and configured to comply with applicable Google Play Families requirements.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the game's online services, advertising configuration, SDK versions, data practices, or applicable legal requirements change.
